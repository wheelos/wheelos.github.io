# WheelOS open-source community structure

## Technical Steering Committee

- **Responsibilities:** technical direction, code quality, release planning and technical decisions.
- **Members:** core contributors, module maintainers and senior technical experts.
- **Working method:** regular technical meetings, pull-request and issue review, and technical sharing with the community.

## Operations Committee

- **Responsibilities:** non-technical community operations including outreach, resource management and events.
- **Members:** community managers, designers, marketing specialists and active user representatives.
- **Working method:** plan hackathons and summits, promote projects, manage funding and sponsor relationships, and maintain collaboration tools such as Slack and forums.

## Contributors

Contributors submit code, documentation, defect reports and optimization proposals; participate in technical decisions through proposals; and share experience through meetings and forums. Collaboration happens through GitHub, Slack and mailing lists.

## Long-term and project work

- The Technical Committee maintains the roadmap and core codebase and runs technical reviews.
- The Operations Committee runs events, updates communication channels and tracks community growth.
- Project-based work prepares releases, implements major modules and runs user research and campaigns.

## Maintaining technical quality

- Publish code style, contribution and technical-decision processes.
- Make meeting notes and discussions public.
- Recognize core contributors.
- Lower participation barriers through sharing sessions, courses and documentation.

## Community outreach

Use LinkedIn, YouTube and relevant regional channels for updates and tutorials; publish blogs, case studies and industry articles; run hackathons and meetups; participate in industry events; and collaborate with universities, companies and developer communities.
