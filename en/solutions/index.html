Featured solution ​

Integrated Autonomous Driving Kit ​

A development kit built around the NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 32G platform, with a 16-line LiDAR, four surround cameras, RTK GNSS + IMU and a baseline autonomous-driving stack. It is designed for teams that need to establish a prototype, validate algorithms and integrate a vehicle in a controlled environment.

Reference price: from RMB 39,900. Final configuration, freight, taxes and optional services are confirmed in a formal quotation.
