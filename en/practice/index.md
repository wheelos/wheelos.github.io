# Practice records

Practice records are the primary evidence source for this knowledge base. Only records with environment, procedure and results can move a topic from `Design guidance` to `Partially verified` or `Verified`. Formal records are indexed in the [evidence index](/en/evidence/).

## Recording rules

- One record answers one falsifiable question.
- A conclusion is bound to versions, hardware and data; avoid context-free "supported" claims.
- Retain failed and unreproduced results.
- Safety-related conclusions must state the test boundary; simulation cannot replace a safety case.
