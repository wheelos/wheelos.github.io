# About Wheelos

Wheelos is dedicated to advancing the field of autonomous driving through open-source collaboration. We believe in the power of community-driven innovation to accelerate the development and adoption of safe and reliable autonomous driving technologies.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to build a thriving open-source ecosystem for autonomous driving by providing high-quality software, hardware, and resources, fostering collaboration among developers, researchers, and enthusiasts worldwide.

## Our Team

Our team is composed of dedicated individuals who contribute their expertise and passion to the project. We have two key committees:

### Technical Steering Committee (TSC)

The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) is responsible for defining the technical roadmap, setting architectural guidelines, and ensuring the overall technical quality of the Wheelos project.
